Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PurffleShorts Studio (“the App”) may handle information when you use it. The operator must review and update this policy so it accurately reflects the final application, hosting, and integrations before publication.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and authorization information: when you connect a social media account, the App may receive authorization data such as access tokens, refresh tokens, account or page identifiers, and permissions granted by you.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the App’s features, establish connections you request, manage or publish content through authorized platform APIs, maintain security, and troubleshoot errors.

If you connect TikTok, Facebook, or another service, information is exchanged with that service as needed to perform the action you request and according to the permissions you approve. Before publishing this policy, the operator must specify exactly where the App stores media, settings, logs, and authorization tokens; whether any remote server or relay receives them; and which service providers can access them. The App should not share personal information except as needed to provide requested features, comply with law, or protect the service and its users.

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. To request deletion of information held by the operator, contact the email below. You may also revoke third-party access in the relevant platform settings. Revoking access may not automatically delete data already held by the platform.

The operator uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the way the App is deployed. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
